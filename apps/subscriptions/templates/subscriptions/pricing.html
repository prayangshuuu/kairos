Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to my bookings and event types if I downgrade?
Your existing bookings and data are never deleted. If your subscription lapses, extra event types beyond the free limit become read-only and hidden from your public profile. When you resubscribe, all your event types are automatically restored exactly as they were.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time directly from your billing settings or via the Stripe Customer Portal. There are no cancellation fees or retention gauntlets.
Do you take a cut of my meeting payments?
If you connect your Stripe account, Kairos charges 0% platform fee. If you use Kairos's PayStation fallback route, a 3% service fee applies to collect funds on your behalf. Read the detailed breakdown in your Payment Settings.
How do BDT subscriptions renew?
BDT plans renew manually. Before each billing cycle ends, you will receive an invoice email with a payment link. You also get a 7-day grace period after period expiry before any plan features pause.
